Meet Our Founders

A Shared Vision for Reading, Writing and Creative Expression.

Rooted in a profound love for literature, Read Pustak is nurtured by an intimate mother-daughter partnership. Together, they bridge seasoned editorial stewardship with the fearless, vibrant creativity of young authorship—crafting a home where stories of all generations are cherished.

edit_note Young Author & Inspiration

Avni Chaturvedi

Young Author | Storyteller | Literary Enthusiast

Avni embarked on her literary voyage at the tender age of ten, inspired by ancient Indian folk fables, mythology, and modern childhood wonder. Her stories echo an uninhibited freshness that speaks directly to peers and grown-ups alike.

Deeply passionate about reading and regional lore, she champions the belief that every young mind holds an authentic story. Her voice is the living spark behind Read Pustak with Avni—an inviting realm built to reassure young readers that their pens hold true creative power.

shield_person Founder & Owner

Neena Chaturvedi

Founder & Owner, Read Pustak Publications

With an abiding commitment to the transformative power of books, Neena guides the institutional heart of Read Pustak. She founded the imprint to cultivate mindful reading habits and creative expression from a child’s earliest formative stages.

She orchestrates publishing operations, editorial curation, school partnerships, and literary circles with meticulous care. Her mission is to create a welcoming, dignified launchpad where aspiring storytellers receive genuine respect, guidance, and lasting wings.
