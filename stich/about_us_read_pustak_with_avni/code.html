About Read Pustak with Avni

A Journey of Reading, Writing and Creative Expression.

Read Pustak with Avni is an inspiring initiative dedicated to encouraging children and aspiring young writers to discover the pure joy of books, embrace the art of storytelling, and realize the power of their creative voices.

Nurturing Young Voices Where imagination meets literary craft

history_edu Heritage & Beginnings

Our Story

The origin of Read Pustak Publications grew from an enduring passion to preserve the beauty of printed literature and kindle children's imagination. As young Avni began writing and illustrating her own whimsical tales at an early age, her creative journey revealed something profound: children hold complete, vibrant universes waiting to be read.

Leadership & Curation

Neena Chaturvedi

Founder & Owner

Leading Read Pustak Publications with administrative rigor and literary dedication, Neena anchors the publication house's production standards, curated selections, and genuine commitment to enriching child literacy without commercial excess.

Heart & Catalyst

Avni Chaturvedi

Creative Inspiration

As a celebrated young author, Avni serves as the living pulse and authentic voice behind Read Pustak with Avni, guiding peers through relatable storytelling, peer workshops, and demonstrating that every child's voice has genuine weight.

Our Purpose

Every program, book release, and workshop is purposefully oriented toward three enduring commitments:

local_library

Encouraging Reading

Helping young readers discover the joy of books, nurturing natural curiosity, and cultivating a lifelong, effortless love for reading beyond formal curricula.

draw

Inspiring Creative Writing

Helping children find their distinct authorial voice, translate untamed imagination into expressive prose, and confidently embrace creative storytelling.

workspace_premium

Supporting Young Authors

Providing compassionate mentorship, rigorous editorial guidance, and authentic, dignified pathways into published literature for budding storytellers.

format_quote
lightbulb Core Philosophy

“Every Child Has a Story Worth Sharing.”

At the foundational core of Read Pustak with Avni is the belief that every child deserves genuine opportunities to read, imagine, write, and express themselves freely. Children see the world with rare wonder, untainted perspective, and instinctive empathy—qualities the world desperately needs to read and cherish.
