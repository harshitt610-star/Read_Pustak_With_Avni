About the Initiative

Literary Havens & Creative Workshops

Read Pustak with Avni is an editorial and cultural initiative born under Read Pustak Publications. It serves as a nurturing haven where young minds discover the joy of reading, master the craft of storytelling, and gain the confidence to publish original literature.

Through guided reading circles, interactive author sessions, and child-centered publication programs, we bridge generational knowledge with modern, youthful perspectives.
